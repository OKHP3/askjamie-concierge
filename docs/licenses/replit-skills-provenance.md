development guidance. They are not public catalog entries and do not acquire
catalog trust or publication approval by being installed here.

(OverKill Hill P³), imported by the repository owner in commit `36f411f`.

## Historical evidence boundary

Imported evaluation JSON, benchmark JSON, `evals/results.md`, and the
task-executor evolution review retain their original names, paths, hashes,
dates, quotations, and observations. They describe upstream versions and are
historical records only. They do not validate the renamed or modified AskJamie
packages, and their old paths must not be used as local execution instructions.
Visible evaluation cases may inform new tests, but results must be recorded as
new evidence against the actual adapted revision. The telemetry runner and its
schema/tests remain executable development tools; its README gives the current
local invocation. Use separate output paths for new runs to preserve the
historical records.

These legal notices and clearly delimited historical evidence are the narrow
exception to the AskJamie-only naming rule. Active guidance uses AskJamie or
neutral terminology and derives repository coordinates from the environment.
