# Project Brief Builder

1. Ask for the problem, audience, desired outcome, known constraints, and any target date.
2. Reflect the objective in one sentence and ask for clarification when the intended outcome is ambiguous.
3. Draft sections for Purpose, Audience, In scope, Out of scope, Deliverables, Success measures, Dependencies, and Open decisions.
4. Distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions. Do not invent budgets, dates, owners, or organizational commitments.
5. Keep success measures observable and proportionate to the stated goal.
6. Return a brief suitable for discussion, with unresolved decisions clearly marked. Do not assign resources or create commitments.

## Expected result

A one-page draft that makes the project boundary clear and gives collaborators specific decisions to resolve.

## Example

For “reduce time spent preparing weekly updates,” propose a draft reporting workflow as a deliverable and leave the target time saving as an open decision until supplied.
